Contact

Nitro Pitch is made by an independent developer who reads every message. Here is the best way to get in touch for each kind of question.

Last updated November 5, 2026

Feedback, ideas and bug reports

The fastest way to reach the developer is the feedback button (the chat bubble at the top of the main menu). Choose whether it is a bug, an idea or something else, write a short description and send it. You can send feedback without leaving the game, and replies from the developer appear in the game as a message. Helpful feedback can even earn you bonus XP.

When you report a bug, it helps to include:

  • what you were doing when it happened (for example, the game mode and what you clicked),
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead,
  • the device and browser you were using.

The game also sends an automatic error report when something goes wrong, which helps the developer fix problems you might not notice.

Reporting a player

To report a player for cheating, offensive chat or an offensive name, click their name to open their player card and choose Report. Write the reason and send it; reports from chat include the messages around the report so the developer can see what happened.

Ban appeals

If your account was banned and you believe it was a mistake, send a message with the feedback button from a signed-in account and explain what happened. Please be polite and specific.

Privacy and data requests

For questions about your data, see the privacy policy, which explains what is collected and how it is used. You can also send a privacy question with the feedback button. Advertising and cookie choices can be changed at any time on the cookies page.

Before you write

Many common questions are already answered in the FAQ, and new players will find everything they need to get started in how to play.
