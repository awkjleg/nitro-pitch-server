Garage and items

Your car is yours to customise. Every item in Nitro Pitch is cosmetic: it changes how your car looks, never how it drives, so nobody can buy an advantage. Here is what you can collect and how to get it.

Last updated November 5, 2026

What you can customise

ItemWhat it does
Car bodyThe shape of your car. There are more than twenty bodies, from the all-round Octane to the flat Dominus and the long Breakout. All bodies use the same handling.
PaintThe main colour of your car. Pick any colour you like with the colour picker, for free, plus a paint finish such as matte, metallic or pearlescent.
DecalA pattern on the body: stripes, flames, lightning, camo, carbon fibre and many more.
WheelsThe rims and tyres.
BoostThe trail your car leaves when you boost.
Goal explosionThe effect that goes off in the net when you score.
Topper and antennaA hat on the roof and a flag or ornament on an antenna, including flags of many countries.
Title and bannerThe title under your name and the banner behind it on your player card.

Painted cars

Painted cars are special versions of the car bodies with coloured trim: the bumpers, skirts, spoiler and mirrors are painted in one of fourteen colours, from Crimson and Sky Blue to Gold, Black and the rarest of all, Titanium White. A painted car is a separate item from the normal car, and you can still choose any main paint colour for it. Painted cars come only from drops.

Rarities

Items have a rarity that shows how hard they are to get: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very Rare, Import, Exotic and Black Market. The rarity colour appears on the item's tile in the Garage.

How to get items

Levelling up

Every match gives XP. Levels go up to 100, and many items unlock at set levels. Every five levels you also get a drop.

Drops

A drop is a sealed crate of a certain rarity. Open it in Nitro Pass > Drops and it reveals a random item of that rarity that you don't already own, sometimes upgraded to the next rarity. You get drops from:

  • every fifth level you reach,
  • reaching a new rank for the first time,
  • every daily and weekly quest you complete,
  • Nitro Pass tiers,
  • and, now and then, a lucky bonus drop after any match.

Quests

There are three daily quests and four weekly quests, such as winning matches, scoring goals or making saves. They count in online matches and in matches against bots. Each quest gives XP, Nitro Pass XP and a drop, and harder quests give better drops: daily quests give Rare or Very Rare drops, and weekly quests give Very Rare, Import or Exotic drops. If you don't like one of today's daily quests, you can reroll it once a day.

The Nitro Pass

The Nitro Pass is a free season track with fifty tiers. Everything you do in matches earns pass XP, quests earn extra, and each tier unlocks an item, a drop or bonus XP.

Trade In

Got items you don't use? In Nitro Pass > Drops > Trade In, put in five items or unopened drops of the same rarity and get one random item of the next rarity up.

Trading with other players

Drop items, painted cars and items you were given can be traded with other players who are online. In the Players online list, press Trade next to their name, then each of you picks up to eight items to offer. The swap only happens when both players accept the same offer, and changing an offer cancels the acceptance, so nobody can switch items at the last second. Items you unlock by level, rank or the Nitro Pass can't be traded.

Clans

Join or create a clan in the Career menu. Clan members share a clan tag in front of their names, a clan colour and clan XP earned from matches, and higher clan levels unlock clan decals.
