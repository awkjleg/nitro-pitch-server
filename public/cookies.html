Cookies & Browser Storage

See what Nitro Pitch saves in your browser, why it is used, and how to manage optional advertising.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser and sent with relevant web requests. Local storage keeps data for this site between visits; session storage normally lasts for the current tab session. Both can support gameplay without being advertising cookies.

The table below describes storage used by the game code. Providers may also use their own storage when you use sign-in or load pages that include advertising.

2. What the game stores

First-party browser storage used by Nitro Pitch
Storage / examples Purpose Duration
np-settings, np-mute Controls, camera, audio, and settings. Until changed or cleared.
np-profile, np-name, np-train, np-tut Local progress, name, training records, and tutorial state. Until replaced or cleared.

nitropitch_terms_accepted* Remember terms acknowledgment in this browser, including account-specific records where available. Until cleared.

Local storage has no built-in expiry. Game code can replace or remove entries earlier. Firebase Authentication can also use browser persistence, including IndexedDB, to keep you signed in.

3. Advertising & third-party services

Nitro Pitch includes Google AdSense. The game currently requests the AdSense script when the game page opens. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers for ad delivery, personalization, fraud prevention, and measurement. Cookie names and lifetimes vary by product and provider.

Learn more from Google’s advertising information, Google’s ad cookie reference, and Google’s ad settings. Sign-in uses Firebase, hosting uses Cloudflare, and the game loads some resources from external providers. These requests can share technical information such as your IP address independently of advertising personalization settings.

The repository does not include a separate analytics loader. Advertising measurement is part of optional advertising. A hosting provider or deployment configuration may add services outside this repository; this page should be reviewed when those change.

4. Advertising & privacy controls

You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and Google’s partner-site ad settings. These settings are managed by Google; their availability depends on your account, browser, and location.

Your browser can block third-party cookies and clear stored site data. Blocking cookies or turning off personalization does not necessarily remove all ads or stop every advertising request.

The current game requests Google AdSense on page load. This policy page does not itself block advertising scripts or record an advertising consent preference. If a consent message is displayed by Google or another provider, use the choices offered there.

Terms acknowledgment is not consent to advertising cookies. Browser controls and Google settings are separate from the game’s terms popup.

5. Browser controls & deleting data

Use your browser’s privacy settings to review or remove cookies and site data, block third-party cookies, or use private browsing. Look for “Cookies and site data” or “Site settings” for the Nitro Pitch address.

Clearing site data may sign you out and delete guest progress, settings, saved replays, and consent choices. It does not delete account data held on servers. Make sure any progress you want to keep is saved to your account first.

If browser storage is blocked, the game may not remember your choice between visits. Advertising remains off unless you actively allow it during that visit.

6. Updates & questions

We will update this page when storage or advertising practices change. For questions, use the feedback speech-bubble button at the top of the game menu (sign-in required). See the Privacy Policy for personal-information requests and the Terms & Conditions for game rules.
