How to get started

  1. Open Nitro Pitch in a modern browser with WebGL graphics support.
  2. Open Settings to check the keyboard or controller controls.
  3. Try the tutorial or free play without an account, then sign in when you’re ready for online matches. Both are available from Play > Practice.

Customize your car in the Garage. Earn XP through matches and quests, explore clans in Career, and open More for recent match history and blocked players. Find daily and weekly quests in Nitro Pass.

Browser game questions

Looking for unblocked game websites?

Nitro Pitch is a browser game website you can visit directly at nitropitch.app. It needs no game download. Whether it is unblocked depends on your school, workplace or network settings; access is not guaranteed on every network.

Is Nitro Pitch free to play?

Yes. You can play the game in your browser for free. The site has advertising controls explained on the Cookies page.

Can I play with other people?

Yes. The game offers online multiplayer, parties and private rooms. You can also play exhibition matches against bots.

Where do I find the new player features?

Choose More from the main menu. Match history shows your latest 20 completed matches, and Blocked players manages chat and invitation blocks. History and blocks are saved for your account on the current device.

What if the game cannot connect?

Check your connection and the server status indicator. Online matches need access to the game server. If your browser cannot start the 3D game, check that WebGL is supported and enabled.
