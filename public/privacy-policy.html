1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your username, email address, account details, support messages, and any information you share while using the game. We may also collect technical details such as device type, browser information, IP address, gameplay activity, crash or error data, ad interaction data, and analytics information related to our services.

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help operate the game, serve ads, analyze performance, and support account functionality.

2. Cookies, Local Storage & Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to recognize your device, maintain account sessions, remember gameplay settings, support login or consent preferences, and measure the effectiveness of ads and analytics. These technologies may also be used by our advertising partners. See the Cookies & Browser Storage Policy for the storage inventory and advertising controls.

  • Essential cookies help keep the game functioning and maintain sessions.
  • Preference cookies remember settings and consent choices.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand usage patterns and improve the game.
  • Advertising cookies may be used by Google AdSense and third-party ad partners.

3. Google AdSense & Required Advertising Disclosures

Our game includes Google AdSense, whose script is requested only after you allow advertising through our cookie controls. These services may use cookies and related technologies to display personalized or non-personalized advertisements based on your interests, app usage, or your visits to websites participating in the Google advertising network.

Google may use data collected through advertising cookies to measure ad performance and personalize ad delivery. You can learn more about Google’s advertising practices and privacy controls here: Google Advertising Policies.

Advertising partners may receive information such as device identifiers, browser information, ad interaction data, and approximate usage or location data so they can deliver relevant ads or measure campaign performance.

4. Advertising Partners

We may work with ad networks, measurement providers, and third-party services to deliver advertising, support analytics, and improve the player experience. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, or measure ad effectiveness.

  • Ad networks may use cookies or identifiers to serve ads.
  • Measurement providers may track conversions and ad performance.
  • Third-party vendors may use data to personalize or improve relevant advertising.

5. Data Collection & Use

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and improve gameplay and account features.
  • Maintain secure login and account management.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or cheating.
  • Measure app health, usage trends, and ad performance.
  • Support customer service and respond to account issues.
  • Ensure legal, security, and operational compliance.

6. Third-Party Services & Domain Migration

We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, authentication, crash reporting, support tools, warnings, and advertising. These services may process some data on our behalf under their own privacy policies. When the site moves from nitropitch.bibleway.workers.dev to nitropitch.app, a browser migration can copy saved game data and readable sign-in persistence to the new address. A single-use encrypted transfer is temporarily held on our Cloudflare server for up to five minutes. The decryption key stays in your browser tab, and the server copy is deleted after redemption or expiry. The old browser copy is kept; existing destination saves are protected from silent replacement. See the cookies and storage page for limitations and controls. We limit the information shared to what is necessary for the service to function and use reasonable safeguards where available.

7. Children’s Privacy

Nitro Pitch is not intended for children under the age of 13 without appropriate parental consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for advertising or marketing purposes. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data without valid parental consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete or restrict that information.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal information, or to object to certain forms of processing. If applicable, you may also request information about the data we maintain about you. To exercise those rights, contact the site owner through the game support or contact information provided on the website.

9. Terms & Your Choices

The Terms & Conditions describe account responsibilities, fair play, and community rules. The game may ask you to acknowledge those terms and this privacy notice. Acknowledgment is remembered in browser storage; it is not consent to optional advertising.

The cookies page lets you allow or reject advertising separately and change your choice later. Terms acknowledgment does not allow advertising cookies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here and may be shown to users before they continue using the game. Continued use of the game after changes are posted indicates your agreement to those changes.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, cookies, advertising disclosures, or your personal information, contact the site owner using the support or contact details available on the game or website. We will review requests in line with applicable privacy laws and respond in a reasonable timeframe.
